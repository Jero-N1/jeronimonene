Proyecto 01

Tayrona Archaeological Pavillion

TipoTrabajo grupal
InstituciónUniversidad Nacional de Colombia
UbicaciónSanta Marta, Colombia
Año2025
Concepto

This pavillion rests on the bay between two mountain formations, where its central and elevated position on a rugged topography allows the Tayrona ecosystem to flow beneath the pavilion and maintain its continuity. The building adopts extended forms, where the horizontal volume never exceeds two levels in height, thus avoiding dominance over the landscape and adapting to the terrain's curves through modules that stretch like arms to anchor themselves without altering it.

Conceived as a modular fabric, the building unfolds its internal structure following a mat-building logic, where horizontal and multidirectional extension is articulated through the arrangement of courtyards that, besides introducing light and ventilation, allow the architecture to fragment and reconfigure without losing coherence. These voids act as living cores of the project, regulating the thermal conditions of the warm climate and promoting a gradual transition between interior and exterior.

The concept is reinforced by a stilt-based strategy that raises the structure on concrete piles, regularizing the ground without modifying it and ensuring visual and ecological permeability.
