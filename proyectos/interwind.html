Proyecto 03

Interwind Pavillion

TipoConcurso de arquitectura
ConvocatoriaArquine Mextrópoli 2026
UbicaciónCiudad de México, México
Año2026
Entrelazar(se)

Faced with the scarcity of pause spaces in public areas, the pavilion proposes a refuge of calm that integrates local identity through materiality and morphology inspired by Mexican values. For this reason, the pavilion is conceived from the residues of Mexico City: the plastic sacks that wrap fruits and vegetables in the markets and construction material waste. These elements are transformed into the foundation of a temporary structure that questions waste and proposes a new use for what the city discards.

The piece, composed by seven recycled wooden porticos arranged in a radial segment, rests on the plaza as a threshold between the Palacio de Bellas Artes and the Beethoven monument, opening towards them. It presents itself as a respite from the dynamism of the city, a meeting point that invites citizens to contemplate their surroundings, collaborate with one another, and generate community ties and roots. Inside, the weaving is interrupted to facilitate multiple passages and circulation, while on its surface, built with plastic sacks, it encourages users to linger.

Its weavings, arranged as a skin that connects the structure, represent a direct link between artisanal tradition and the contemporary life of the city — intertwining materials that are born and die in an attempt to recover their identity.
